The Defensive Driving Series

Our defensive driving series is delivered fully online. Drivers work through short, interactive lessons on a phone, tablet or computer, whenever it suits them, without taking time off the road.

Hazard Awareness

Scanning the road ahead, spotting hazards early and planning an escape route before you need one.

Space & Following Distance

Managing the space around your vehicle, safe following distances and stopping distances for heavy loads.

Fatigue & Driver Wellbeing

Recognising fatigue, managing long hours safely and staying alert on long-distance routes.

Adverse Conditions

Driving safely at night, in rain, wind and poor visibility, and adjusting to road conditions.

Vehicle Checks

Pre-trip inspections and spotting defects before they become incidents.

Rules of the Road

Your responsibilities as a professional driver and staying compliant with road traffic regulations.
