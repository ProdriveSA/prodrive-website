DRIVER HEALTH & WELLNESS

A trained & healthy driver is a safer driver

Skill without health is a risk on the road. Health without training is a risk for all road users.

Picture a driver suffering a stroke behind the wheel of a fully loaded 56-ton GCM truck and trailer. No amount of driving skill can prevent what happens next. But regular health screening can pick up the warning signs, like high blood pressure, high cholesterol and high blood sugar, long before they become a crisis on the road.

Proud to support Trucking Wellness

Trucking Wellness is a primary healthcare programme for workers in South Africa's road freight and logistics industry, an initiative of the National Bargaining Council for the Road Freight and Logistics Industry (NBCRFLI). Drivers can access free health services and screening at more than 20 Roadside Wellness Centres on major trucking routes and at border posts, as well as at mobile wellness centres.

Our founder championed the Trucking Wellness programme while at Daimler Trucks and Buses. Today ProDrive SA brings health and wellness into driver training, encouraging drivers and fleet owners to make regular screening part of being road-ready.

About ProDrive South Africa

ProDrive South Africa (Pty) Ltd is a specialist commercial driver training company, focused on truck and bus driver development, with growing solutions for rigid delivery vehicles, smaller commercial vehicles and delivery motorcycles. We work with transport, logistics and passenger transport operators in the private and public sectors.

Rooted in decades of automotive and commercial vehicle experience, we understand the challenges fleet operators face, and we now combine proven practical training with online learning and digital vehicle inspection solutions.

Drivers today manage systems, not just loads.

Commercial vehicles are more technologically advanced than ever. A driver is no longer someone who simply moves goods from A to B, but a professional responsible for complex vehicles and systems. Megen Naidoo

Owner & Sole Director

Megen brings 35 years of automotive industry experience to ProDrive SA, including 19 years with Mercedes-Benz South Africa. A qualified motor technician, Megen spent seven years at the Mercedes-Benz manufacturing facility as a Quality Auditor, carrying out static and dynamic testing of customer-ready commercial and passenger vehicles, before moving to head office in Pretoria.

There, Megen managed the Driver Training portfolio for Daimler Trucks and Buses for around a decade, delivering driver, product and soft-skills training to dealers and fleet customers, with a thorough understanding of manufacturer compliance, policies and procedures.

That combination of technical knowledge and hands-on training experience is the foundation of everything ProDrive SA does.
