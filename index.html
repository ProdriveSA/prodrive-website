The Defensive Driving Series

14 modules · online learning · practical assessment

Our defensive driving programme is moving online. Drivers will work through short, interactive lessons on a phone, tablet or computer, followed by a practical assessment with a ProDrive assessor.

  1. 01Introduction to Defensive Driving
  2. 02Road / Traffic Rules and Regulations
  3. 03Vehicle Safety Inspection
  4. 04Hazard Recognition & Collision Prevention
  5. 05Defensive Driving Behaviour
  6. 06Driver Responsibilities & Attitude
  7. 07Space Management
  8. 08Emergency Situations & Accident Prevention
  9. 09Economical Driving
  10. 10Driver Risk Management – Rollovers & Fatigue
  11. 11Adverse Weather & Night Driving
  12. 12Following & Stopping Distances
  13. 13Observation, Awareness & Blind Spots
  14. 14Health, Nutrition, Drugs & Alcohol
