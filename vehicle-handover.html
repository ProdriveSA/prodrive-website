The walkaround

Six stations around the truck, then into the cab

Each station shows the features in that area and the benefit to the driver or the fleet, with photos, short videos and voice-overs. It is a features-and-benefits walkaround, not a compliance inspection.

    Who it is for

    Built for the people who sell, buy and drive the truck

    Manufacturers and dealers

    • The same complete handover at every dealership, every time.
    • Shows off the features that sell your trucks.
    • Helps drivers use the vehicle's systems correctly from day one.
    • A link you can send to prospective buyers before they visit.

    Fleet owners

    • New and relief drivers learn a truck before they drive it.
    • Drivers know how to use fuel-saving and driver-assistance systems.
    • No need to take drivers off the road for a classroom session.

    Drivers

    • Learn at their own pace, on their own phone.
    • Watch again whenever they need a reminder.
    • Voice-overs in English, Afrikaans or isiZulu.
